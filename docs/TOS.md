## 1. What Bind is

Bind provides software and a **cover window** over opt-in covered credits backed 1:1 by USDC in a vault (when configured).  
Clawback-enabled assets are a **claims execution rail**, not a consumer stablecoin brand.

Bind is **not**: a bank, a licensed insurer (unless separately authorized), a money market, or a guarantee of partner-protocol solvency.

## 3. Opt-in risk

Cover applies only to **unsettled** lots inside window `T`.  
After settle or unwrap, transfers are final under Bind rules.  
You may pay an Exit Pool fee to leave cover early.

## 4. Partner protocols

If you use Bind via a wallet or DeFi protocol (e.g. Blend-like Safe Deposit):

- That partner’s own terms also apply.  
- Bind does **not** insure that partner’s smart contracts, oracles, or liquidations.  
- Bind’s liability, if any, is limited as in the Cover Schedule (vault/pool assets).
